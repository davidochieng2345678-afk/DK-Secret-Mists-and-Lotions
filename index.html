Chapter 1 — Introduction
Why I Wrote This Book
By David Kiruo · Nairobi, Kenya 🇰🇪

I Almost Gave Up at 2AM

I almost gave up. It was 2 AM in Nairobi, the city finally quiet outside my window, and I was staring at a Google Analytics screen showing zero visitors for the third month in a row. I had built a website. I had written content. I had done everything the YouTube tutorials told me to do. And yet — nothing. No traffic. No clients. No results.

I remember sitting there thinking: maybe SEO is only for people in America. Maybe it doesn't work here in Kenya. Maybe the internet was built for them — not for us. That night, I nearly closed my laptop and walked away from the whole thing forever.

That moment of self-doubt is exactly why I wrote this book.

Because nobody told me the truth when I was starting out. Nobody gave me a clear, honest, step-by-step guide that spoke to my reality — someone building a digital presence from Nairobi, working with a limited budget, trying to reach both local Kenyan clients and international markets, in a continent where most SEO content completely ignores us.

Every course I bought was made for someone in London or Los Angeles. Every blog post assumed I had a team, a big budget, and an audience that already trusted me. Nobody was speaking to the Kenyan entrepreneur sitting alone at their desk at 2 AM wondering why Google couldn't see them.

I had to figure it out the hard way.

I spent years reading every SEO blog I could find, buying courses, watching hundreds of hours of tutorials, testing strategies on my own websites, failing spectacularly, adjusting, and slowly — very slowly — learning what actually works. I made expensive mistakes. I wasted months on tactics that were already dead. I chased rankings that never came. I wrote content nobody read.

But then things started to shift.

One morning I woke up to an email from a business owner in Westlands, Nairobi, asking how I had ranked above companies ten times bigger than mine. Then came a message from a client in the UK who had found me on Google. Then a call from a Kenyan e-commerce brand whose organic traffic I tripled in just 90 days. Then another client. Then another. Then another.

SEO had changed my life — and I had finally figured out how to make it work from Africa.

That is when I knew I had to write this book.

I wrote it because when I was sitting at that desk at 2 AM, frustrated and confused, I wish someone had handed me exactly what you are reading right now. A complete, no-fluff, practical guide — not written for Silicon Valley, not written for a London agency — but written for you. The entrepreneur in Nairobi trying to get found online. The freelancer in Mombasa building a digital career. The student in Kisumu who knows the internet is the greatest opportunity of our generation. The small business owner anywhere in Africa who is tired of paying for ads and getting nothing lasting in return.

SEO is not magic. It is not reserved for the wealthy or the technically gifted. It is a skill — and like every skill, it can be learned, practised and mastered. I am living proof of that. And after reading this book, you will be too.

This book covers everything. From the very basics of how Google works, all the way to running a profitable SEO business from Kenya. Every chapter is written from my personal experience. Every strategy has been tested in the real Kenyan market. Every mistake I warn you about is one I made myself so you don't have to.

Welcome to SEO Mastery: From Nairobi to the World.

Let's get started.

— David Kiruo, Nairobi 🇰🇪
